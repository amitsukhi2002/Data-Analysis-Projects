### 🛍️ Global Retail Sales Analytics

### 📌 Project Overview

This project delivers a robust, programmatic retail data analytics solution utilizing global transactional datasets from Maven Analytics. Built entirely in Python, the project automates the processing, cleaning, and exploratory analysis of high-volume international e-commerce data. The ultimate goal is to uncover hidden purchasing behaviors, map seasonal demand curves, evaluate international profit margins, and provide stakeholders with actionable, data-driven supply chain strategies. 

### 📈 Project Domain & Function

* **Domain:** E-commerce & Global Retail Data Science
* **Functional Scope:** Automated ETL processing, multi-country sales tracking, inventory performance indexing, and customer purchase-frequency modeling.

### 🛠️ Tools & Tech Stack

* **Language:** Python
* **Data Manipulation:** pandas (for high-performance data framing, grouping, and indexing) & numpy (for fast vectorised math operations).
* **Data Visualization:** matplotlib & seaborn (for engineering polished statistical charts, distribution plots, and correlation heatmaps).
* **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook / Python Scripts (.py).

### 📂 Data Engineering & Analytical Pipeline

### 1. ⚙️ Automated ETL & Data Cleaning

* **Schema Enforcement:** Standardizing data types across multi-year retail transactions, dealing with mismatched string layouts, and extracting date elements (Year, Month, Quarter, Day of Week).
* **Missing Value Management:** Programmatically identifying and isolating null rows in product parameters or shipping codes to prevent analytical distortion.

### 2. 📈 Sales & Temporal Trend Analysis

* **Time-Series Tracking:** Rolling aggregates and month-over-month (MoM) growth calculations to visualize clear retail seasonal cycles and peak shopping holidays.
* **Volume Analysis:** Isolating top-performing product categories and sub-categories to identify the core cash cows versus slow-moving inventory lines.

### 3. 🌍 Geographic & Market Segments

* **Regional Contribution:** Grouping transaction arrays by country and continent to evaluate where market demand is densest.
* **Margin Inspection:** Calculating net profit yields across different international distribution corridors to spot regions with high logistical overhead.

### 4. 👤 Customer Basket Mechanics

* **Order Statistics:** Statistical distribution mapping of Average Order Values (AOV) and Units Per Transaction (UPT) using boxplots and histograms.
* **Correlation Auditing:** Heatmap visualizations testing the correlation between pricing strategies, discount depths, and total items ordered.

### 💡 Strategic Recommendations

* **Dynamic Inventory Balancing:** Automate stock-level alerts based on historical Python time-series trends to prevent stockouts in high-demand regions during peak seasons.
* **Targeted Pricing Interventions:** Leverage localized profit margin insights to deploy localized discount or bundling strategies without eroding overall brand profitability.
* **Logistics Streamlining:** Focus shipping and distribution optimization efforts on regions identified with lower margin yields to decrease overhead and stabilize cross-border returns.
