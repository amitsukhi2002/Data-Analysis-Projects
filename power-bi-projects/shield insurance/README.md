### 📊 Shield Insurance Analytics | Power BI Capstone

### 📌 Project Overview

This business intelligence pilot was engineered for Shield Insurance Ltd. during a virtual internship at AtliQ Technologies. The project addresses a critical enterprise challenge: a lack of unified, automated analytics to monitor revenue growth, customer behavior, and sales execution across disparate geographic regions and demographics. This custom analytical reporting system bridges that operational gap, transforming raw tracking data into automated, data-driven visibility.

### 📈 Project Domain & Function

* **Domain:** Insurance & Risk Management Analytics
* **Functional Scope:** Revenue pipeline optimization, policyholder demographic tracking, sales channel performance management, and claim settlement rate analysis.

### 🛠️ Tools & Tech Stack

* **Primary Platform:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Prep & Source:** Microsoft Excel & Power Query (Advanced ETL processing, data cleansing, and schema validation)
* **Calculations:** Advanced Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) focusing on Time Intelligence functions and dynamic KPIs.
* **Architecture:** Robust Data Modeling establishing optimized relational schemas between customer, policy, and financial tables.

### 📂 Analytical Views & Core Focus Areas

### 1. 👥 Customer Trends & Demographics

* **Age Bracket Segments:** Behavioral tracking across age brackets to isolate high-value age categories and identify low-penetration youth sectors.
* **Customer Acquisition:** Dynamic Month-over-Month (MoM) growth metrics monitoring the velocity of active policyholder acquisition.

### 2. 💰 Revenue Channels & Sales Execution

* **Channel Distribution:** Market share evaluation comparing the performance of traditional offline agents against digital and online application platforms.
* **Asset Performance:** Strategic auditing of individual insurance policies to identify top-performing flagship assets.

### 3. 🗺️ Geographic Market Analysis

* **Regional Market Density:** Spatial evaluation mapping total policyholders and gross revenue pipelines to rank geographic performance across city clusters.

### 4. 📉 Operational Claims Deep-Dive

* **Settlement Rate Fluctuations:** Tracking historical claim settlement variances by demographic to optimize risk models and evaluate operational exposure across age tiers.

### 💡 Strategic Recommendations

* **Digital Transformation:** Formulate targeted digital marketing campaigns to elevate the market share of lean online applications and reduce heavy operational reliance on offline agent channels.
* **Product Tailoring:** Design entry-level, cost-effective insurance products specifically tailored for the 18–25 age group to bridge the current gap in customer volume and low claim settlement velocity.
* **Geographic Expansion:** Replicate the high-yield distribution and marketing playbook utilized in dominant city markets across emerging regions to drive scalable expansion.
